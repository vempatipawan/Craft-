Maximum six per order. We bake what is ordered, so the number is real.

Ingredients & allergens

Maize starch, rice starch, water, sunflower oil, sugar, psyllium, yeast, sea salt, citrus fibre, natural flavouring.

Contains: none of the 14 major allergens. Produced in a bakery that handles nuts and sesame.

How it is made
Laminated by hand over two days, proved overnight at 4°C and baked each morning before the shop opens. No gluten means no gluten network, so the lift comes from the lamination and the timing rather than from flour behaving itself.
Keeping & warming
Best on the day. After that, four minutes at 180°C brings the layers back. They freeze well for a month, straight from the bag into the oven.
