Craftsmanship

A bakery that happens to be gluten free.

Gerry and Nilüfer opened the first Craft on 5 June 2020, around the corner from the Albert Cuyp market. The idea was simple and slightly stubborn: stop making gluten-free bread that tastes like an apology.

Everything is laminated, proved and baked by hand in our own bakery. There is no wheat flour on the premises, so there is nothing to cross-contaminate. That is not a claim on a label. It is how the building works.
